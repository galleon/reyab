This is the bill of materials for the NVFP4 builds, which cover both the 64k and 128k context scenarios. On B300 it is two SR680a V4 servers with eight B300 each, three SR655 V3 service nodes and three switches: a redundant pair of 400 gigabit converged switches and one out-of-band management switch. No GPU fabric switch is needed, because each model fits inside one server and talks over NVLink. On RTX PRO 6000 it is six SR675 V3 servers with eight cards each, the same three service nodes, and a pair of 800 gigabit SN5610 switches carrying both the GPU fabric and the converged network, because GLM-5.3 and Mistral Large 4 span several servers. The service nodes run the Kubernetes control plane, Base Command Manager, and the reyab gateway, recorder and monitoring. If you stay in native format, B300 needs a third server at 128k context and RTX PRO 6000 needs ten servers. Rack units and power are estimates; part numbers come from Lenovo's published bills of materials and should be confirmed with Lenovo.
